# Frugal Shopping App - Feature Roadmap & Ideation

This document tracks upcoming concepts, feature requests, and roadmap ideas inspired by Flipp and modern smart grocery assistants to make unit-price shopping even more powerful.

---

## 📋 1. Smart Shopping List & Deal Matcher (Flipp Style)
- [ ] **Keyword & Flyer Matcher**
  - Match items on the shopping list (e.g., *"Greek Yogurt"*) with active deals and digital circulars at nearby grocery stores.
- [ ] **One-Tap Store Grouping**
  - Sort shopping lists by Store and Aisle so shoppers can map their trip and know exactly where to find the lowest unit prices before leaving home.
- [ ] **Trip Total Cost Estimator**
  - Project the expected checkout bill based on the calculated winning unit price deals, showing exact expected savings.

---

## 🏷️ 2. Shrinkflation & Historical Price Tracker
- [ ] **Benchmark "Stock-Up Price" Targets**
  - Set alerts for specific product price thresholds (e.g., *"Olive Oil under $0.25/oz"* or *"Coffee beans under $7/lb"*).
- [ ] **Shrinkflation Alert Engine**
  - Compare current packaging weights/sizes against historic packaging to flag stealth price increases (e.g., box reduced from 18oz to 15oz with no sticker price drop).
- [ ] **Store Price Memory Cache**
  - Store previously scanned price tags by branch, allowing instant cross-comparison (e.g., *"Target price is currently 12% higher than your last ALDI scan"*).

---

## 🛒 3. Multi-Store "Split Trip" Optimizer
- [ ] **Bulk vs. Regular Optimizer**
  - Calculate if a bulk club pack (e.g., Costco) is genuinely cheaper per unit than buying a supermarket's BOGO or multi-buy sale.
- [ ] **Split Trip Calculator**
  - Suggests an optimal multi-stop shopping route (e.g., *"Stop 1 at ALDI for dairy/produce, Stop 2 at Kroger for brand sales & coupons"* to save $30 total).
- [ ] **Spoilage Risk Adjustment**
  - Incorporate product shelf life; adjust the effective unit price of bulk items if a portion is likely to go to waste.

---

## 🎟️ 4. Coupon Stacking & Promo Mechanics
- [ ] **Manufacturer + Store Coupon Combiner**
  - Add digital and manufacturer coupon discounts directly to the scanning calculator to compute exact net out-of-pocket costs.
- [ ] **BOGO & Multi-Buy Promo Engine**
  - Model complex retail promotions (e.g., *"Buy 2 Get 1 Free"*, *"Mix & Match 5 for $0.99"* or *"Spend $25 get $5 back"*) to show genuine unit cost adjustments.
- [ ] **Cash Back & Rebate Integration**
  - Tag qualifying products with cash-back alerts for popular rebate networks (Ibotta, Fetch Rewards, store loyalty accounts).

---

## 👥 5. Live Household Synchronization
- [ ] **Shared Pantry & Cart Sync**
  - Synchronize lists across family members in real-time. Allow one shopper to scan and verify shelf tags in-store while another updates the list at home.
- [ ] **Crowdsourced Store Price Maps**
  - Share anonymized, local, user-scanned shelf prices so nearby shoppers can benefit from real-time community price discovery.

---

## 🍳 6. Recipe-to-Pantry Price Servings
- [ ] **Cost-per-Meal serving breakdown**
  - Import recipe ingredients and automatically calculate the cost per serving based on standard unit-price averages.
- [ ] **Frugal Ingredient Swapper**
  - Suggest budget-friendly alternatives (e.g., *"Canned whole peeled tomatoes are 45% cheaper per ounce than jarred pre-made sauce"*).
